Changelog

What changed in each release. Update to the newest with muhos-update.

MuhOS 0.1.2

v0.1.2 · 25 November 2026

  • Click a dock icon for an app with several windows to see live previews of them above the icon; click one to raise and focus it, like the macOS dock.
  • Clicking a window in the dock, the app menu or Mission Control now always raises it, even when it's floating or was just unminimised.
  • Menu bar: modules chosen for the centre in Settings → Bar show their colours and respond to clicks again.

MuhOS 0.1.1

v0.1.1 · 25 November 2026

  • Apps open in their own systemd scopes: they no longer count as the shell's memory or stop when the shell restarts or you log out.
  • Menu bar shows the modules chosen in Settings → Bar.
  • Dark Style works with colour presets (macOS ↔ macOS Dark) and switches MuhOS's light and dark wallpapers.
  • Music: covers embedded in the file; names the real output device.
  • Mission Control (Super+G, three fingers up).
  • App grid: press and hold to move apps.
  • Translations for MuhOS's own wording; French.

MuhOS 0.1.0

v0.1.0 · 24 November 2026

The first release, the same software as pre-release 9: a macOS-style desktop for Fedora 44 and 45 on Hyprland, installed with one command and updated with muhos-update.

Pre-release 9

v0.1.0-pre.9 · 24 November 2026

  • Other windows no longer freeze while a maximized window is open: zoom makes a window fill the screen as an ordinary window, as on macOS.

Pre-release 8

v0.1.0-pre.8 · 24 November 2026

  • muhos-update, and a choice of how MuhOS updates: manually, a daily notification, or automatically.
  • The installer shows file sizes, the total download and a progress bar for each file.
  • Nothing asks upstream Serpantinum for updates any more.

Pre-release 7

v0.1.0-pre.7 · 24 November 2026

  • Logging out returns to the login screen instead of a black screen.
  • Click to focus, Super+Tab and Alt+Tab, cascading new windows, and minimise with Super+M or the app menu.
  • Apps pinned from a running window open from the dock.
  • The app grid shows apps in pages, as in Launchpad.
  • Settings checks MuhOS releases and its Update button installs them.

Pre-release 6

v0.1.0-pre.6 · 24 November 2026

  • Fixes from the first test on real hardware: screens scale automatically, running apps appear in the dock, a terminal that works without kitty, and wallpapers from anywhere on your disks.

Pre-release 5

v0.1.0-pre.5 · 24 November 2026

  • Fedora 45 support, an equaliser built on PipeWire, an app grid and the one-command installer.

Pre-release 4

v0.1.0-pre.4 · 24 November 2026

  • The macOS look and behaviour: a macOS menu bar, floating windows, and apps with macOS-style window buttons, icons and fonts.

Pre-release 3

v0.1.0-pre.3 · 25 October 2026

  • Frosted glass throughout: the dock, launcher, notifications, on-screen indicator and lock screen.

Pre-release 2

v0.1.0-pre.2 · 19 October 2026

  • MuhOS's own interface: a GNOME-style layout with a transparent menu bar and glass panels.

Pre-release 1

v0.1.0-pre.1 · 18 October 2026

  • The first packages for Fedora 44 with Hyprland, with a MuhOS login session.
